Your profile is what other people see of you in the app: your picture and your name. It lives in the **Profile** section of Settings.

## Changing your picture

Click **Change picture** and pick a JPG, GIF or PNG image up to 1 MB. The new picture shows at once, but it is only kept when you click **Save**.

## Changing your name

Type your full name, at least 3 characters long, and click **Save**. A message confirms when your profile was updated.

## Your email

Your email shows in your profile, but you cannot change it.

> [!NOTE]
> Only an administrator changes an account's email. If yours changed, ask whoever runs the app.
