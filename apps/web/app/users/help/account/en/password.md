## Changing your password

In the **Password** section of Settings, type the new password. The confirmation field unlocks as soon as you start typing: type the same password in it and click **Save**.

The password needs at least 8 characters. Both fields are cleared after saving, and also if something goes wrong.

## A good password

> [!TIP]
> A long, random password you use nowhere else is worth more than a short one full of symbols. A password manager makes one up and keeps it for you.

## If you forgot it

On the sign-in screen, use **Forgot your password?**. You get an email with a link to make a new one.
