## The theme

In the **Appearance and language** section of Settings, pick **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. With **System**, the app follows the mode of your computer or phone. The change applies at once, with no saving.

The same three choices sit in the menu of your picture, at the top right, to switch without leaving the screen you are on.

> [!NOTE]
> The theme is kept on this device. On another computer or browser, you pick it again.

## The language

In the same section, **Language** switches the interface between English and Portuguese, at once. The choice goes with your account: it holds on any device you sign in from. The Help also shows in the language you pick.

On the sign-in screens, before you sign in, the globe icon at the top picks the language.
