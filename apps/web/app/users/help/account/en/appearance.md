## The theme

In the **Appearance** section of Settings, pick **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. With **System**, the app follows the mode of your computer or phone. The change applies at once, with no saving.

> [!NOTE]
> The theme is kept on this device. On another computer or browser, you pick it again.

## The language

The globe icon at the top of the screen switches the interface between English and Portuguese. The Help also shows in the language you pick.
