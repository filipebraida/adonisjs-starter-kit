A role is a set of capabilities: what someone holding it may do. The **Roles** screen, under Administration, shows each role and its capabilities.

## The roles the app comes with

- **Administrator** may do everything: users, roles, impersonating and the activity log.
- **Manager** sees the user list, adds and invites.
- **User** uses the app, with no administration screen.

## Your institution's roles

Besides those, you can make roles named as your institution needs and tick each one's capabilities. Whoever is given the role may do exactly what was ticked.

> [!TIP]
> Give each role only what it needs. Adding a capability later is easier than finding out someone could do more than they should.
