The **Activity** screen, under Administration, records what each person did in the app.

## What each row says

Each row shows **When**, **Who**, **What** (created, updated, deleted…), **On** which record and **From** where (the IP address and the page the person was on). When someone acted while impersonating another person, the row says who was behind it.

## Filtering

Search by the name or email of who acted, or pick a kind of event. In the user list, **View activity** opens this screen already filtered by that person.

> [!NOTE]
> The log is there to understand what happened, for instance when something changed and nobody remembers who changed it. It cannot be edited from the screen.
